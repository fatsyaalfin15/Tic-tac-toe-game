How to Play

  • Enter names for both players (Player X and Player O)
  • Click "Start Game" to begin
  • Player X always goes first
  • Take turns clicking on empty squares
  • The first player to get 3 of their marks in a row (up, down, across, or diagonally) wins
  • If all squares are filled without a winner, the game is a draw
  • Click "Reset Game" to start a new game at any time
